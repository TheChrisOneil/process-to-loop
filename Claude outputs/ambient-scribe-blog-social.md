Introducing the EverBetter Sovereign Health Loop™: from the patient's wrist to the patient's plan.

Maria walks into the clinic for her 10:00 visit. She consented to share her sleep patterns and diet choices through the MED app, so her nurse is ready to talk about last week's sleep. Her physician opens her notes and finds every reading since the last visit.

Maria filled out nothing.

The EverBetter MED app captures her health between visits. That data shapes a pre-visit note for each role. During the visit, 35 protocols, each tuned to the clinician, the patient, and the date, capture what matters. The clinician reviews every extraction. The care plan reaches Maria with zero re-entry and syncs to her own Solid pod.

That is continuity of care. Every visit counts.
